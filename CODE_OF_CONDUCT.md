# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in Ezhunai and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints, backgrounds, and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the collective community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Actively welcoming beginners and learners exploring new fields
- Upholding privacy and respecting members who participate without disclosing personal details

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing private information without explicit, verifiable consent
- Condescending attitudes toward beginners or gatekeeping knowledge
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or community setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces—including GitHub repositories, the Discord server, WhatsApp groups, and any official communication channels—and applies when an individual is representing Ezhunai in public spaces.
