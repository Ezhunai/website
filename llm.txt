# Ezhunai (எழுநை)

> Learn together. Build together. Rise together.

Ezhunai is a Tamil-rooted, globally welcoming peer-to-peer learning community built around a simple belief: everyone has something to learn, and everyone has something to share.

## Overview
- **Name Meaning**:
  - **EZHU (எழு)**: To rise, grow, or move forward.
  - **INAI (இணை)**: To connect, unite, or come together.
  - **EZHUNAI (எழுநை)**: Rise together.
- **Vision**: A world where people can learn, create, and grow together without being limited by background, age, experience, or the subjects they are interested in.
- **Mission**: To bring curious people together in an inclusive, peer-to-peer environment where they can exchange knowledge, explore ideas, develop skills, and collaborate on meaningful projects.
- **Identity**: Rooted in Tamil cultural identity while remaining globally open and welcoming to everyone across languages, backgrounds, and skill levels.
- **Commercial Model**: Free community initiative. Not a paid-course business or conventional school.

## Core Belief & Values
- **Learn → Explore → Build → Share → Grow**
- **Mutual Learning**: Everyone can be both a teacher and a learner.
- **Curiosity**: Questions and exploration matter more than appearing perfect.
- **Inclusivity**: Welcoming beginners, generalists, and learners of all backgrounds.
- **Privacy & Respect**: Free participation without forced personal data collection.

## What Members Explore
- **Technology & Programming**: Software engineering, web development, open-source projects.
- **Art & Design**: Visual design, UI/UX, illustration, creative experiments.
- **Writing & Communication**: One-minute talks, public speaking, storytelling, idea exchange.
- **Business & Problem Solving**: Entrepreneurship, product building, practical problem-solving.
- **Science & General Knowledge**: Research concepts, exploration across diverse fields.
