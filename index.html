OUR BEGINNING

Why we started
Ezhunai ?

கற்றது கைமண் அளவு, கல்லாதது உலகளவு

It reminds us to remain humble and curious, knowing that however much we learn, there is always more to discover and wisdom to gain from one another.

Many young people know their own field well but want to explore more. Someone in technology might want to learn marketing. Someone in design might want to improve their communication skills. Yet finding the right people, environment, and opportunities to try can be difficult.

That’s why we started Ezhunai: a community to learn from each other, share knowledge, explore different fields, build real projects, and gain confidence.

Through workshops, interactive sessions, conversations, games, and collaborative projects, we want to make learning practical, enjoyable, and consistent.

Our goal is to create an environment where people help each other grow.

FAQ

A few good questions.

What if I’m a complete beginner?+

You’re welcome here. Bring your questions and your curiosity. You don’t need a portfolio, a certificate, or prior experience.

Do I need to speak Tamil?+

No. Ezhunai has Tamil roots and welcomes everyone. Shared conversations can use English or another common language so people can participate together.

Does it cost anything to join?+

No. Ezhunai is a free community. Learning and participation don’t depend on paying for access.

What if I’m shy or don’t have much time?+

Start by listening. Ask a question when you’re ready, and contribute when you can. You don’t need to participate every day.

What happens after I join?+

The join button opens our Discord invitation directly. Accept the invite to join, explore the conversations, and introduce yourself when you feel comfortable.
